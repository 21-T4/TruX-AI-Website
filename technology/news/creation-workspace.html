TruX-AI expands its creation workspace

TruX-AI brings focused chat, image generation and personalization together in one workspace.

The free experience includes 15 image generations per month. Pro image generation is positioned for higher-quality creation, while personalization options let users shape how they work with the assistant.

The product is intended to support different kinds of work without turning every task into the same interface.
