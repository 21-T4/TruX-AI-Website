Privacy isn't a setting

It is part of the product.

TruX is built to minimize what we collect and make our boundaries easy to understand. We only use basic account and profile details needed to provide access, support sign-in, and help verify your account.

Free-account conversations are not used for model training. That means your work remains your work—not fuel for improving a model.

✓Purpose-limited account data
Basic information is used for your profile, account verification, and service operation.
✓Google-powered sign-in
Authentication is handled through Firebase using Sign in with Google.
✓Clear by design
