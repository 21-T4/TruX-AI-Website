TruX-Omni

One workspace to think, research and create.

TruX-Omni brings conversation, reasoning, research and visual creation together. Below is everything it does, and what each part is for.

Launch TruX-Omni ↗

Think

Talk it through.

The core of Omni is a conversation that stays useful from the first question to the final draft.

01

Conversational AI and reasoning

Ask in plain language and work a problem step by step. Explanation, drafting, analysis and brainstorming all happen in one continuous chat, so each follow-up builds on what came before.

Good for: planning, writing, learning a new topic.

02

Automatic model selection

You do not have to pick a model for every task. Omni can choose a suitable one for what you ask, so a quick question and a deep reasoning problem both get appropriate handling without touching settings.

Good for: switching between topics without friction.

03

Research and analysis

Explore a subject, compare options, and analyze text or information you provide. Omni helps turn scattered findings into a clear summary you can keep building on.

Good for: reports, comparisons, study notes.

Create

Turn ideas into visuals.

Creative tools live in the same workspace as the conversation, so there is no second product to switch to.

04

Image creation

Describe what you want and generate images right next to your chat. A concept you just discussed can become a visual in the same place.

Good for: concept art, illustrations, mockup ideas.

05

Video creation

Use the same prompt-first flow to create video. Idea, script and footage stay in one workspace instead of being scattered across tools.

Good for: short clips and visual storytelling.

06

Media storage

Supported generated media is kept in cloud storage so your creations remain available to you. It is used for media assets only, not for your chat history.

Good for: coming back to your creations later.

Make it yours

A workspace that adapts to you.

07

Personalization

Set your own system instructions, formatting preferences and workspace behavior, so answers arrive in the style and structure you prefer.

Good for: consistent tone, format and level of detail.

08

Memory

Omni can carry your preferences forward, so you spend less time repeating context at the start of each conversation.

Good for: ongoing projects and recurring tasks.

09

Browser-stored chat history

Your conversations are saved in your own browser rather than on TruX servers. The trade-off is that history belongs to that browser and device.

Good for: keeping conversations close to you.
