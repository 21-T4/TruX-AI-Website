TruX-Code

A coding workspace that knows your repository.

TruX-Code is a dedicated environment for software engineering, kept separate from general chat. Here is everything it offers, and what each part is for.

Launch TruX-Code ↗

Repositories

Work on real code.

The heart of TruX-Code is the connection between the AI and your GitHub projects.

01

GitHub integration

Connect your GitHub repositories and branches directly, so the AI works with your actual project instead of pasted snippets.

Good for: starting from the code you already have.

02

Repository and file analysis

TruX-Code reads the project tree and individual source files to understand how the codebase is organised before it suggests anything.

Good for: onboarding to unfamiliar code, finding where things live.

03

Multi-file changes

Plan an edit that touches several files, review it, and apply it as one atomic multi-file commit. Related changes land together instead of piece by piece.

Good for: refactors, renames, features that span files.

Intelligence

Control how it thinks.

04

Model picker

Choose from several Gemini models served through Vertex AI, depending on whether you want maximum capability or maximum speed.

Good for: matching the model to the task.

05

Thinking levels

Set the AI to think at Low, Medium or High depth. Low answers quickly; High spends more effort on hard problems.

Good for: trading speed against depth per request.

06

Streaming responses

Answers appear as they are written, so you can start reading right away instead of waiting for the full reply.

Good for: staying in flow on large answers.

Extend

Connect the tools around your code.

07

Plugins

Connect apps such as Gmail, GitHub, Slack and Notion through Composio. Once connected, they become tools the AI can use in your chats, and you can switch each plugin on or off whenever you like.

Good for: pulling context from, or acting in, the tools your team already uses.

08

Careful with irreversible actions

The AI is instructed to ask for confirmation before irreversible actions. Plugin connections belong to your account only, and the integration key stays on the server.

Good for: using connected apps with peace of mind.

Workspace

Comfortable for long sessions.

09

Focused layout

A collapsible sidebar, a calm welcome screen and a rounded composer keep attention on the work and out of menus.

10

Themes and accent colour

Choose light, dark or system appearance and set your own accent colour.

11

Synced chat history

Chats are saved to your account and cached locally, so conversations follow you across devices.
